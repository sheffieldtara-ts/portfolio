KPI Visualisation

Making one chart
do the work of five

Role Product Designer, end-to-end
Company SafetyCulture · Analytics
Platform Web (responsive web-view in app)
Status Released

Overview

Customers used KPI thresholds to judge performance at a glance — but charts supported only a single KPI, with no custom ranges or colour coding. I designed multiple-KPI support across SafetyCulture's chart library, letting customers track several targets and performance bands in a single chart.

The problem

Problem → Opportunity Poor UX forcing workarounds → unlock $770K ARR

Without multiple KPIs or custom thresholds, customers couldn't tailor dashboards to their reporting standards or analyse performance across categories. They resorted to two workarounds: creating several near-identical charts, or exporting data to spreadsheets and doing the analysis outside the platform. Both were time-consuming and undermined the value of Analytics itself.

Demand was well-evidenced: the need surfaced repeatedly in customer calls and in feedback tracked on the product feedback board, tied to the revenue opportunity above.

The solution

Building on the existing single-KPI functionality, I designed a KPI configuration modal with two modes:

  • Single-value KPIs — up to 4 per chart, each shown as a line indicator at its chosen value, with custom names and colours set in the modal.
  • Range KPIs — up to 5 performance bands defined by min/max values, displayed as either the range values or their custom labels.

The design extended the platform's existing behaviour — bar and column colours changing to show whether values were met — to support multiple targets at once, keeping the mental model customers already had. KPI visualisation worked across seven chart types: stacked, column, grouped, bar, line, timetable, and dial.

Outcome

Released to all customers on web. Post-release impact wasn't instrumented, so the honest answer is: shipped, impact not tracked — a gap I'd push to close earlier if I ran this project again.
