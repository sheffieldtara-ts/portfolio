How I design

I believe the best problems to solve sit at the intersection of desirability, feasibility, and viability — solving the right problem before solving the problem right. That means working as a triad with engineering and product, staying closely connected to customers through research (moderated calls, usability testing) and building relationships across Customer Success, Support, GTM, and Marketing to understand where design can have the most impact.

Most recent experience

Within SafetyCulture's Analytics domain, I led data visualisation work — modernising how customers create, visualise, and act on their data. I shipped Item Scores, a page/section/question scoring feature that had sat as one of Analytics' most-requested gaps on ProductBoard and Slack (81 customer requests, ~$1M AUD ARR opportunity), and was part of the team behind the platform's earliest AI investigation, validating real world usage alongside customer appetite.

Alongside Analytics, I rebuilt SafetyCulture App's mobile editing experience natively for iOS and Android — usability tested before launch.

What I bring

Stakeholder management and communication

I'll flag a deadline at risk before it slips, share a differing view with a manager, and explain a design decision to a customer in plain terms, not jargon. When it matters, I'll be upfront with senior stakeholders too — while trusting that their experience carries real weight and deserves to be part of the call. With my team specifically, that means daily updates on where projects are at, surfacing blockers the moment they show up, and asking questions early instead of losing time going at it alone.

Cross-functional collaboration

I work as a triad with product and engineering day to day, then reach wider: lifecycle and comms on growth strategy, GTM and Customer Success on what customers are prioritising, customer support for direct insight from the frontline, and product marketing on customer-facing docs.

Leading with trust and vulnerability

I show up with vulnerability, transparency, and trust — people do their best work when they're supported in what they're good at, not judged for what isn't their strength. I try to bring that same care to how I work with colleagues, customers, and stakeholders. I'm genuinely open to constructive feedback — it's how I get better — and I try to create that same space for others: open, respectful, and safe to share an opinion.

Where I've come from

13 years of design experience (spanning brand, advertising, and product) underpins all of this, sharpened by a UX/UI Design course through General Assembly Sydney in 2018. Along the way I built SafetyCulture's Design Team DNA — now the team's primary onboarding reference across Product and Design.
