How I design

I believe the best problems to solve sit at the intersection of desirability, feasibility, and viability — solving the right problem before solving the problem right. That means working as a triad with engineering and product, staying closely connected to customers through research (moderated calls, usability testing) and building relationships across Customer Success, Support, GTM, and Marketing to understand where design can have the most impact.

Most recent experience

Within SafetyCulture's Analytics domain, I led data visualisation work — modernising how customers create, visualise, and act on their data. I shipped Item Scores, a page/section/question scoring feature that had sat as one of Analytics' most-requested gaps on ProductBoard and Slack (81 customer requests, ~$1M AUD ARR opportunity), and was part of the team behind the platform's earliest AI investigation, validating real world usage alongside customer appetite.

Where I've come from

13 years of design experience (spanning brand, advertising, and product) underpins all of this, sharpened by a UX/UI Design course through General Assembly Sydney in 2018. Along the way I built SafetyCulture's Design Team DNA — now the team's primary onboarding reference across Product and Design.

What I bring

Research before opinion

TARS wasn't a quick survey — 18 interviews across 24 stakeholders in 7 industries before any design decision got made.

Frameworks other teams can reuse

TARS didn't just answer where insights should go — it produced a reusable Intent × Urgency decision rule the whole team can apply to future problems, not just that one.

Design connected to business numbers

Item Scores and KPI Visualisation were both sized in ARR and request volume alongside the design work, so prioritisation isn't just a craft opinion.

Systems, not just screens

Team culture

I lead with vulnerability, transparency, and trust. People do their best work in environments that harness what they're best at, instead of ridiculing what isn't their strength — a safe environment lets people be their best selves. I try to live that in every interaction, with colleagues, customers, and stakeholders alike.
